Which beaches are sheltered?

—

A beach is sheltered when the wind blows off the land (offshore). "Facing" is the direction you look when you stand on the sand looking out to sea. Shore colours use the wind at each spot and the way the coastline faces there; tall headlands and cliffs can give extra shelter the map doesn't know about. Wind lines and arrows show where the wind is blowing to.
