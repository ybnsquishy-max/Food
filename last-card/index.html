🎮 Object of the game

Be the first to play every card in your hand. When one player goes out, the round ends and points are counted from the cards left in hands.

🃏 Card values

  • Number cards (0–9): face value
  • Skip, Reverse, Draw Two: 20 points each
  • Wild, Wild Draw Four: 50 points each

🎯 Your turn

  • Match the top card of the discard pile by colour, number or symbol.
  • A Wild can be played any time; you choose the next colour.
  • A Wild Draw Four can only be played when you have no card matching the current colour. The next player may challenge it.
  • If you can't play, draw one card. If it can be played, you may play it straight away.
  • Say "UNO" when you play your second-last card. If someone catches you first, draw 2.

⚡ Action cards

  • Skip: the next player misses a turn.
  • Reverse: play changes direction. With 2 players it acts like a Skip.
  • Draw Two: the next player draws 2 and misses a turn.
  • Wild Draw Four: choose the colour; the next player draws 4 and misses a turn.

🏁 Two ways to keep score

  • Lowest total wins (common at home): each player adds the points left in their own hand. When someone passes the limit (often 500), the lowest total wins. This is how your score sheet works.
  • Highest total wins (official rules): the player who went out collects the points from everyone else's hands. First to 500 wins.
  • Pick the one you play on the Game tab under "Winner is".

💡 House rules (optional)

  • Stacking: answer a Draw Two with a Draw Two (or Draw Four with Draw Four) to pass the penalty on.
  • Jump in: play an identical card (same colour and number) out of turn.
  • Seven-O: playing a 7 swaps hands with a player you choose; playing a 0 passes every hand in the direction of play.

Importing replaces the scores of anyone with the same name. New names are added as players. ELO ratings only come from rounds saved on the Game tab.
